Changes and returns

Can I cancel my order?

If the order was not processed yet, you can cancel it with no costs. Except for custom orders or gift cards.

Can I change or return my order?

You can change or return your order, up to 14 days of delivery. Except for custom orders or gift cards.

We can refuse the return, if the products don't meet the returning criteria.

How to cancel or return my order?

To iniciate the process, you should contact us via online@debarro.pt.

Refund

When we get and inspect the order, the refund will be processed in up to 14 days.

If you are located outside of Portugal, the returning shipping costs are not refunded.
