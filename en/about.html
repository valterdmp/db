About us

Our history

DeBarro Ceramics was created in the city of Barcelos, a region historically linked to the art of ceramics.

The company was founded by two young artisans, who belong to the third generation of a family dedicated to handicrafts.

The productive process of the pieces is completely manual, which makes them unique with incomparable finishes.

The aim is to give a new meaning to the tradition of pottery, keeping it alive. In this way, the contemporary spirit is aligned with the traditional, guaranteeing the originality of the products created.

We act conscientiously and transparent, through our open-source website, zero waste of raw material and the use of sustainable and recycled materials from local suppliers.
